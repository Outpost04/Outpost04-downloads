OUTPOST04 — PRIVACY INFORMATION
Publisher: Outpost04
Updated: 11 November 2026
Contact: https://github.com/Outpost04/Outpost04-downloads/issues

LOCAL PROGRESS
Progress, unlocks, settings, daily rewards and goals stay on your computer in the
existing "Mit Spil" user-data folder. Launcher installations and selected versions
are stored separately under "Outpost04Launcher". Updates preserve progress.
These files are not cloud backups. Local logs and service caches may contain
your display name or session identifiers; do not post your whole save folder.

ONLINE CO-OP
Epic Online Services provides device-based sign-in, lobbies and relay transport.
It receives device/service identifiers and connection information needed to run
these features. The current game does not require an Epic account or launcher.
Lobby members receive your display name, character/skin, loadout and gameplay
state/statistics. Public server listings show the chosen server name, invite
code, player count, difficulty and wave. Internet play uses Epic relays. The
separate LAN/direct-IP options use direct network connections.

CHAT AND VOICE
Text chat is sent to your current lobby. A short in-memory history is cleared
when you leave. Voice is sent while voice is enabled and you hold push-to-talk
(C by default). Releasing the key, disabling voice or leaving stops microphone
capture. The game does not save voice recordings. Other players may record what
they hear or see; avoid sharing sensitive information.

DOWNLOADS AND OPTIONAL SIGN-IN
Public downloads and updates do not require GitHub sign-in. GitHub and its
content-delivery services receive normal connection/request information.
Optional GitHub sign-in can help with request limits and older private downloads.
If you choose it, saved tokens use Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain.
If secure storage fails, login stays in memory for that session only. SIGN OUT
removes stored login when the OS service succeeds. Access can also be revoked
in your GitHub account settings. Existing private launchers require an update
to remove their sign-in requirement.

DIAGNOSTICS AND CONTROL
COPY DIAGNOSTICS is optional and does not send anything automatically. Its report
contains version/install checks and numeric connection results, not login tokens
or saves. If you open a public support issue, its contents are public. Never post
passwords, tokens, personal information or unreviewed logs.
You can change your display name and voice settings. Uninstalling game files does
not automatically delete progress. Back up your saves before deleting local data.
Deleting local files does not delete records held by Epic or GitHub; those services
handle data requests and retention under their own policies.
